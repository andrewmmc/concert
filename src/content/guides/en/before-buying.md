Before paying, confirm that the ticket listing matches the show you intend to attend.

## Check these details

- The venue and performance date
- The seating layout and stage position
- Any restricted-view or age-limit notice
- Whether the ticket is digital, mailed or collected in person

Keep the order confirmation and use only the organiser's official links for later updates.
