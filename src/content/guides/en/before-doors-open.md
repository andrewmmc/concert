Journey times can increase quickly when thousands of people arrive together. Work backwards from the door time rather than the advertised show time.

## Allow extra time for

- Crowded trains, platforms and station exits
- The walk from the station to the venue
- Security and bag checks
- Merchandise and collection queues
- Finding the entrance printed on your ticket

Check the organiser's latest notice on the day because entrance and security arrangements can change by event.
