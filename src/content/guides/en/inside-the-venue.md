Keep your ticket available after entry in case staff need to check your section. Find your seat and the nearest washroom before the venue gets dark.

## Before the performance

- Confirm your section, row and seat number
- Put your phone on silent
- Follow the event's photography and recording rules
- Keep aisles and emergency exits clear

Ask venue staff if the ticket details or signage are unclear.
