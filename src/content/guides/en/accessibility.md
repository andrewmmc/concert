Accessible routes and seating arrangements vary by venue and event. Contact the ticket seller or organiser early when you need a specific arrangement.

## Confirm in advance

- A step-free route from transport to the entrance
- The designated accessible entrance
- Wheelchair spaces and companion seating
- Any assistance available from venue staff

Check again shortly before travelling in case lifts, routes or event arrangements have changed.
