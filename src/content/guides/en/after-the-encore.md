Leaving often takes longer than arriving because the whole audience moves at once. Follow venue instructions and do not stop in exit routes.

## Plan the journey home

- Check the last train or bus before the show
- Expect one-way walking routes and temporary barriers
- Save an alternative station or bus route
- Choose a meeting point away from the main exit

Allow extra time and follow instructions from venue and transport staff when crowd controls are active.
